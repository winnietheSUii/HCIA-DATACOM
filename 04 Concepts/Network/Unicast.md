# Unicast

Traffic sent from one sender to one receiver. In Ethernet, destination is a single [[MAC Address]] with I/G bit = 0.

## Behavior
- Switch forwards unicast to specific port if MAC is known
- Unknown unicast is flooded within the VLAN until learned
