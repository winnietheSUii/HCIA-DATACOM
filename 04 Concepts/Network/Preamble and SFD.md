# Preamble and SFD

Synchronization fields at the start of an [[Ethernet Frame]].

## Preamble
- 7 bytes of 10101010 pattern
- Allows receivers to lock onto sender's clock

## SFD (Start Frame Delimiter)
- 1 byte of 10101011 pattern
- Marks the boundary where Destination MAC begins
