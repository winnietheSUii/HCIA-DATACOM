# LAN (Local Area Network)

A network that spans a small geographic area, typically a single building or campus.

## Characteristics
- High bandwidth (typically 100 Mbps - 10 Gbps)
- Low latency
- Low cost per device
- Privately owned and managed

## Common Technologies
- Ethernet (IEEE 802.3)
- Wi-Fi (IEEE 802.11)
