# WAN (Wide Area Network)

A network that spans a large geographic area, connecting multiple LANs across cities, countries, or continents.

## Characteristics
- Lower bandwidth compared to LAN
- Higher latency
- Uses leased lines or public infrastructure
- Often involves ISP services

## Common Technologies
- MPLS
- Frame Relay (legacy)
- PPP/PPPoE
- SD-WAN
