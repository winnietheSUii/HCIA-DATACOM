# MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

A network that spans a metropolitan area, such as a city or large campus, bridging the gap between LAN and WAN.

## Characteristics
- Covers 5-50 km range
- Higher speed than WAN
- Often uses fiber optic
- Typically owned by consortium or ISP
