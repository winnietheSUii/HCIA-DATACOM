# Ethernet Standards

Key IEEE 802.3 Ethernet standards by speed and media.

| Standard | Speed | Media |
|----------|-------|-------|
| 10BASE-T | 10 Mbps | Copper UTP (Cat3/5) |
| 100BASE-TX | 100 Mbps | Copper UTP (Cat5) |
| 1000BASE-T | 1 Gbps | Copper UTP (Cat5e/6) |
| 1000BASE-SX/LX | 1 Gbps | MMF/SMF fiber |
| 10GBASE-SR/LR/ER | 10 Gbps | Fiber |
| 10GBASE-T | 10 Gbps | Copper (Cat6/6a) |
| 25G/40G/100G/400G | Various | Fiber (SR4/LR4/ER4), DAC/AOC |

## Notes
- "BASE" denotes baseband signaling
- Suffixes indicate medium and reach
- Auto-negotiation commonly selects speed/duplex on copper
